    2	## Knowledge — one policy for every role
    3	
    4	The KB stores findings that would be costly to rediscover: research results, non-obvious
    5	external behavior, hard-to-find constraints, empirical comparisons, failed approaches and
    6	why a non-obvious decision was made. It is not a description of the current repository.
    7	
    8	Before adding a finding, ask: what would another capable agent otherwise have to investigate
    9	or test again? Time spent alone is not evidence of value. Do not add general engineering
   10	advice, a paraphrase of accessible documentation, function/file inventories, current settings,
   11	status reports or a list of your edits. Read changing code/config directly. A code-related
   12	entry belongs only when it preserves an expensive experiment, rejected alternative or design
   13	reason that cannot be recovered by ordinary source reading.
   14	
   15	### Find the relevant experience
   16	
   17	Use the injected topic index, then search exact symbols, errors or distinctive terms in the knowledge base and task reports:
   18	`rg -n -i -F -e '<symbol>' -e '<symptom>' .orchestra/kb .orchestra/tasks`.
   19	Read the matching section with its conditions and evidence. Historical behavior is a hypothesis
   20	for today's version, not a current guarantee. If necessary, follow sources in the task or
   21	pinned Git history (`kb/history.md` when present). Skip lookup when the named code/command
   22	already answers the question. Search `.orchestra/kb/` and `.orchestra/tasks/` with `rg`; no matches do not prove that an idea is new.
   23	
   24	When reporting a negative result such as "nothing found", "no new records" or "no errors" after
   25	searching, traversing logs, collecting data or fanning out, state the coverage: which source or
   26	channel was checked, how many records or files were inspected, and whether the result is complete,
   27	cut off by a limit, or affected by an error. A zero without this coverage is not a result.
   28	
   29	### Preserve the knowledge, not another transcript
   30	
   31	Write a concise, self-contained explanation in ordinary Markdown. Retain the observation,
   32	material conditions (version/environment/date when relevant), the unsuccessful attempts,
   33	and the reason for the conclusion. Distinguish measured behavior from interpretation and
   34	untested hypotheses. "Failed in this experiment" does not mean "never works".
   35	Link to inspected evidence or a recorded experiment; do not make up missing provenance.
   36	There is no required card, status vocabulary, fact ID, receipt, section count or line format.
   37	One exception (owner decision, 2026-10-18): an entry about the effect of a change (a prompt,
   38	rule, mechanism or model route) states a verdict, `helped`, `hurt`, `no observed effect` or
   39	`confounded`, resting on a contrast between runs before/after or with/without the change, and a
   40	`cue`: the situation in which to apply the lesson. Without such a contrast write `confounded`, or
   41	no entry; an invented cause is worse. Other findings need neither. Reason: in a published
   42	agent-harness study (arXiv 2610.14224) editing the base harness directly, without contrasted
   43	runs, lowered the result, and its framework keeps a lesson only when runs can be contrasted.
   44	
   45	Search for an existing entry first. Another confirmation extends its conditions/evidence;
   46	a contradicting experiment qualifies or replaces the conclusion. Keep the decisive reason
   47	for rejecting an approach. Do not append duplicate versions across topics. A new topic needs
   48	a distinct recurring research question and one index line in `.orchestra/kb/README.md`:
   49	`- [name](topic.md) — description with search terms`.
   50	
   51	Verified reusable findings may update the KB within the authorized task. Unchecked candidates
   52	stay in the task or short personal notes; neither automatic promotion nor a separate approval
   53	receipt is required. New shared behavioral policy still needs the instruction owner's decision.
   54	
   55	Task results, unfinished work and detailed evidence belong in the existing report under
   56	`.orchestra/tasks/<id>/`. Personal observations belong in `.orchestra/workers/<your-name>.md`
   57	only when useful and not already in the KB; these files are injected into your prompt, so
   58	keep them short. No new finding means no memory entry. Research completion does not require
   59	adding a KB line. Do not make daily summaries, compulsory pre-compaction chronicles or a
   60	second record of the same result. Update changed unfinished task state in its existing place.
   61	TODO contains actionable follow-ups, not every observation or declined idea.
   62	
   63	Keep source evidence retrievable when consolidating: pinned reachable Git history may replace
   64	old checkout copies after authorized, verified cleanup. A summary alone does not prove complete
   65	extraction. Check changed links and never publish private credentials from the source material.
   66	</knowledge>
