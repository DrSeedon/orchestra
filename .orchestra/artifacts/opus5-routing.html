What changes

Move Claude roles

Opus 5 low (58.13%) is already tied with Opus 4.8 max (58.97%) at one-eighth the API-equivalent cost. Medium reaches 68.90%; high reaches 72.83%.

Anthropic explicitly calls out judgment, multi-agent coordination, verification, long-horizon consistency, and vision—the traits Orchestra uses.

Do not replace the Sol fleet

Sol max and Opus 5 high are tied on refreshed DeepSWE (72.67 vs 72.83), while Sol consumes a separate, underused Codex subscription pool.

Moving routine workers to Opus 5 converts spare Codex capacity into pressure on the already scarcer Claude pool.

Effort ladder

Medium by default

Orchestration, research, citations, prompt work and normal vision. It reaches 68.90% on DeepSWE at roughly half high's API-equivalent cost.

High for coding pressure

Codex outage fallback, failed Sol tasks with a quality reason, and ambiguous long-horizon implementation. Never fail over when either Claude 5h or weekly meter is near its limit.

With healthy Codex, text/code leaf work stays on Spark/Sol. Opus low is limited to outage fixed-schema extraction, Claude-only screenshot inventory, outage prompt formatting, or an outage ≤2-file patch with an exact test. Max is unused; high vs max is statistical noise. *70% is a proposed guardrail that reserves 30 points in both windows, not a provider threshold.
